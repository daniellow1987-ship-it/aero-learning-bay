How to play. Each card describes a job that needs an angle, a position or a speed sent or measured, either on an aircraft (✈ Aircraft) or in everyday life (⌂ Everyday). Decide which device does it: a torque synchro, a control synchro servo, a differential synchro, a resolver, an LVDT or a tachogenerator. Drag each card into its bin, or tap a card and then tap a bin. Press Check: cards in the right bin lock in green and you'll be asked why. Wrong cards come back with a hint.
