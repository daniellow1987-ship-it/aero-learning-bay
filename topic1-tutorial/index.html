What to notice

  • The SCR is fired part-way through each positive half cycle, when the anode is positive.
  • It switches itself off at the end of that half cycle, when the current falls below IH and the anode goes negative. No off switch is needed.
  • It never conducts in the negative half cycle.
  • Move the firing angle and watch the lamp: this is how a dimmer or a variable-speed control works.

Try this

  1. Keep the load fixed and increase C. What happens to the ripple?
  2. Keep C fixed and make RL smaller, so it draws more current. What happens?
  3. Switch to half-wave. Why is the ripple worse?

Ripple ≈ Iload ÷ (fripple × C). The ripple frequency is 50 Hz for half-wave and 100 Hz for full-wave. Getting it low means choosing the capacitor and knowing the load.
