How to play. Each card describes a transistor at work, either on an aircraft (✈ Aircraft) or at home (⌂ Everyday). Some cards are readings you might take with a meter. Decide whether the BJT is switched OFF (cutoff), switched ON (saturation) or amplifying (active region), or whether the job needs a FET input. Drag each card into its bin, or tap a card and then tap a bin. Press Check: cards in the right bin lock in green and you'll be asked why. Wrong cards come back with a hint.
